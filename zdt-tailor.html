Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how ZDT Tailor ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Customer name, address, and mobile number
  • Measurement data and reference photos
  • Bill/invoice history
  • Order and delivery status information

2. How Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using SQLite database. No data is uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any cloud server.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third parties. The only external service used is Google AdMob for displaying ads.

4. Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to serve banner ads. AdMob may collect the device's Advertising ID to serve personalized ads.

5. Camera & Gallery Access

The app may request access to your device's camera and photo gallery to capture measurement reference photos. These photos are stored locally.

6. No Account Required

ZDT Tailor does not require any user account, registration, email, or password.

7. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

Email: hello.zdtsupport@gmail.com
