FAQ

Pricing Questions

What's included in the Starter plan?

The Starter plan covers a landing page or simple single-page website with responsive design, contact form, basic SEO, and one revision round. It's ideal for freelancers, personal brands, or businesses that need a quick online presence.

How does payment work?

For Starter and Growth plans, we take 50% upfront to begin work and 50% on delivery. For Enterprise projects, we set up milestone-based payments so you only pay as work gets completed and approved.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Absolutely. Many clients start with a Starter or Growth plan and then expand as their needs grow. We'll credit part of your original investment toward the upgrade.

Do you offer ongoing maintenance or hosting?

Yes. After your project launches, we offer monthly maintenance and support plans. We also help you set up hosting (Vercel, Netlify, AWS) — these are separate costs but we'll guide you to the best affordable options.

What if my project needs are between plans?

No problem. These plans are guides — every project is unique. Contact us with your requirements and we'll put together a custom quote that fits your exact scope and budget.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work with clients globally. Communication happens via email, WhatsApp, or video calls — whatever works best for your timezone and workflow.
