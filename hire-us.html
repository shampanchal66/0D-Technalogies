FAQ
Common Questions
How long does a project take?

Depends on scope. A landing page: 3-5 days. A full website: 2-4 weeks. A mobile app: 4-8 weeks. We'll give you a timeline before starting.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Absolutely. We work with clients globally. Communication happens via email, WhatsApp, or video calls — whatever works for you.

What's your payment structure?

50% upfront to start, 50% on delivery. For larger projects, we can split into milestone payments.

Do you provide hosting and domain?

We can help you set up hosting (Vercel, Netlify, AWS) and domain. These are separate costs — we'll guide you to the cheapest reliable options.

Can you fix or improve my existing website/app?

Yes. We do redesigns, bug fixes, speed optimization, and feature additions. Share what you have and we'll assess it.
