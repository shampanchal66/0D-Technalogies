Privacy Policy

zdt.tailor

Last updated: June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how 0D Tailor ("we", "our", or "the app") handles your information when you use our mobile application (package: zdt.tailor).

1. Information We Collect

The app stores the following information locally on your device:

  • Customer name, address, and mobile number
  • Measurement data and reference photos
  • Bill/invoice history
  • Order and delivery status information

2. How Data Is Stored

All data is stored locally on your device using SQLite database. No data is uploaded, synced, or transmitted to any cloud server. You have full control over your data at all times.

3. Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transmit your data to any third parties. The only external service used is Google AdMob for displaying banner ads.

4. Advertising

This app uses Google AdMob to serve banner ads. AdMob may collect the device's Advertising ID to serve personalized ads. You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising in your device settings. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

5. Camera & Gallery Access

The app may request access to your device's camera and photo gallery to capture and attach measurement reference photos. These photos are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded anywhere.

6. No Account Required

0D Tailor does not require any user account, registration, email, or password. There is no login system — all data remains on your device.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

This app is intended for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13.

8. Data Security

Since all data is stored locally, security depends on your device's own protection mechanisms. We recommend using a screen lock on your device.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected within the app and on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at hello.zdtsupport@gmail.com.
