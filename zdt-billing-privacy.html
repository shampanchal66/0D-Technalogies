Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ZDT Billing ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our Android application (package: com.panchalsham.zdtbilling). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about our data practices.

By using ZDT Billing, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

ZDT Billing is designed to be a local-first application. We do not collect, transmit, or receive any personal data on our servers.

1.1 Data You Enter Locally

The app stores the following information locally on your device using SQLite database:

  • Customer information: names and mobile numbers you enter for billing purposes
  • Product/stock data: product names, prices, quantities, and stock levels
  • Bill/invoice history: all bills created within the app
  • App preferences: language selection and other settings stored via AsyncStorage

1.2 Advertising ID

Google AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID to serve relevant ads. This is collected by Google, not by us. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalized advertising in your device's Google Settings.

We do NOT collect, transmit, or have access to any of your data. Everything stays on your device.

2. How We Use Your Information

Since we do not collect any data, the information you enter is used solely for the app's core functionality on your device — creating bills, managing products, and tracking stock. No data is sent to us or any third party (except anonymized ad data via AdMob as described below).

3. Data Storage & Security

All data is stored locally on your device using SQLite database and AsyncStorage. There is no cloud sync, no remote servers, and no data transmission. Data security depends on your device's own protection mechanisms. We recommend using a screen lock and keeping your device software updated.

4. Third-Party Services

The only third-party service integrated into this app is Google AdMob for displaying banner advertisements. AdMob is a service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may collect anonymized data as described in Google's Privacy Policy. We do not control how Google uses this data.

No other third-party services have access to your data. There are no analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, or tracking libraries.

5. Advertising

This app displays banner ads via Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's Advertising ID to serve personalized ads. You can control personalized advertising by:

  • Resetting your Advertising ID in Google Settings
  • Opting out of ads personalization in your device settings
  • Following Google's ad settings at adssettings.google.com

For more details, see Google's Privacy Policy.

6. No Account Registration

ZDT Billing does not require any user account, registration, email address, or password. There is no login system. You can use the app immediately after installation without providing any personal information.

7. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

This app is intended for general audiences, specifically small business owners and shop operators. We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the app, please contact us immediately so we can investigate.

8. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device and we do not collect anything, you have full control over your data. You can:

  • Delete any data within the app at any time
  • Uninstall the app to remove all stored data from your device
  • Contact us with any questions about data handling

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page and within the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
